Synapse Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 August 2026

Applies to Synapse (com.cortavia.synapse)

Who we are

Synapse is developed by Cortavia. Contact: contact@cortavia.in. Website: https://cortavia.in.

Summary

Synapse is a smart SMS organizer. It helps you find, group, and clean up messages on your phone. SMS stays on the device. Synapse is not a Cortavia cloud messaging service and does not require a Cortavia account.

Data we collect

We do not upload your SMS, contacts, or personal data to Cortavia servers.

Processed only on your device:

  • SMS threads used to show your inbox and groups
  • Keyword group rules and optional auto-delete settings
  • Contact names used to label senders

Permissions

  • SMS: Read, receive, and (when Synapse is the default SMS app) send or delete messages.
  • Contacts: Resolve display names for senders.

Sharing and third parties

We do not share Synapse user data with third parties. The app does not include ads or analytics SDKs that collect personal data. Google Play may process install data under Google's policies.

Data retention and deletion

Uninstalling Synapse removes the app and its local settings. Your SMS inbox remains in Android unless you deleted messages while Synapse was the default SMS app.

Children

Synapse is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
