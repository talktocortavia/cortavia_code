Inventory Management Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

Applies to Inventory Management (Tile Warehouse, com.cortavia.tilewarehouse)

1. Who we are

Inventory Management (Tile Warehouse) is developed by Cortavia. Contact: contact@cortavia.in. Website: https://cortavia.in.

2. Summary & Privacy Guarantee

Inventory Management is a local stock inventory tool designed for tile businesses, warehouses, and shops. It lets you organize inventory by size, category, design name, and finish variants (LT, HC, DK, FL), attach sample photos, and log Sell / Buy movements.

100% Offline & Local: Inventory Management requires no cloud account, no invoices, and no backend servers. All photos, counts, and stock movement logs are stored strictly on your phone.

3. Data Processed on Your Device

We do not collect, track, or share any business inventory counts, tile images, or client records.

All data is processed strictly on your handset and never transmitted to Cortavia servers:

  • Inventory Catalog: Sizes, categories, design names, finish options, box quantities, and sell/buy movement logs.
  • Tile Photos: Captured sample photos and gallery selections are stored solely in the application's local documents sandbox.
  • Preferences: Dark mode preferences and custom tile dimension presets.

4. Android Permissions

  • Camera: Allows you to photograph physical tile patterns, surface finishes, or warehouse stock.
  • Photos / Media: Allows you to pick pre-existing tile sample images from your photo gallery.

The app does not access your contacts, SMS, financial data, or location.

5. Sharing and Third Parties

We do not share Inventory Management data with third parties. The app contains no advertising SDKs, marketing trackers, or third-party analytics services.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all data resides exclusively on your phone, uninstalling the application permanently deletes all local inventory logs and captured tile photos. You maintain complete ownership of your inventory data.

7. Children's Privacy

Inventory Management is a professional inventory utility and is not directed at children under 13.

8. Changes to this Policy

Any updates to this policy will be posted here with a revised "Last updated" date. Continued use of the application constitutes acceptance of any updates.
