Family CFO Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 August 2026

Applies to Family CFO (com.cortavia.familycfo)

Who we are

Family CFO is developed by Cortavia. Contact: contact@cortavia.in. Website: https://cortavia.in.

Summary

Family CFO reads bank and UPI SMS on your phone to show income, expenses, and upcoming payments. It does not require a Cortavia account. Transaction data is stored on your device. We do not upload your SMS or ledger to Cortavia servers.

Data we collect

We do not collect, sell, or share your name, email, contacts, location, or SMS with Cortavia or advertisers.

Processed only on your device:

  • Bank and UPI SMS used to build your local transaction list
  • Account last-4 digits discovered from those messages
  • App settings such as appearance

Permissions

  • Read SMS / receive SMS: Used only to import bank and card transaction messages you choose to sync. Messages stay on the device.

Sharing and third parties

We do not share Family CFO user data with third parties. The app does not include ads or analytics SDKs that collect personal data. Google Play may process install data under Google's policies.

Data retention and deletion

Uninstalling Family CFO removes the app and its local database. You can also stop SMS access from Android settings at any time.

Children

Family CFO is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
