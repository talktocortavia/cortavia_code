Finance Trail Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

Applies to Finance Trail (com.cortavia.financetrail)

1. Who we are

Finance Trail is developed by Cortavia. Contact: contact@cortavia.in. Website: https://cortavia.in.

2. Summary & Privacy Guarantee

Finance Trail is an on-device personal and family finance ledger for Indian bank and UPI SMS messages. It reads transaction SMS notifications, converts them into a private ledger, and surfaces monthly income, expenditures, and upcoming autopay/EMI deduction reminders.

Zero Cloud Uploads: Finance Trail does not require an account, has no cloud database, and does not upload your SMS, transaction records, bank names, or account balances to any server. All processing and machine learning classifiers run 100% locally on your smartphone.

3. Data Processed on Your Device

We do not collect, sell, or share personal data such as your name, email, contacts, photos, or location.

The following information is processed strictly on your phone and never transmitted to Cortavia:

  • Transaction SMS: Read-only parsing of bank, card, and UPI alert SMS messages to build your local financial ledger.
  • Masked Account Numbers: Last 4 digits only (e.g. XX1234) used locally to group accounts.
  • Local SQLite Database: Encrypted on-device SQLite database storing your categorized transactions.
  • Local Machine Learning: On-device TF-IDF Logistic Regression classifiers and regex extraction engines that run without internet access.

4. Android Permissions

  • Read SMS / Receive SMS: Needed exclusively to import bank, card, and UPI transactional messages you sync. All messages remain on your phone.
  • Post Notifications: Used optionally to remind you of upcoming autopay and EMI deductions.
  • Internet: Standard platform permission for dependency loading and checking documentation links. No financial data is ever transmitted over the network.

5. Sharing and Third Parties

We do not share Finance Trail user data with third parties. The app does not include third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral trackers, or analytics SDKs that collect personal data. Google Play may process download and crash statistics under Google's standard developer terms.

6. Data Retention, Export, and Deletion

All data stays exclusively on your phone. You have full control over your financial records:

  • Export: Settings allows exporting your categorized transaction dataset as CSV at any time.
  • Clear Data: You can clear the local database directly in the app or from Android Settings.
  • Uninstall: Uninstalling Finance Trail completely wipes the local SQLite database and all cached records.

7. Children's Privacy

Finance Trail is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this Policy

If this policy is updated, the changes will be published here with a revised "Last updated" date. Continued use of the application constitutes acceptance of any updates.

9. Contact
