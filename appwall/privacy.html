App Wall Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 August 2026

App Wall

On-device app firewall · com.cortavia.appwall

Who we are

App Wall is developed by Cortavia. Contact: contact@cortavia.in. Website: https://cortavia.in.

Summary

App Wall is an on-device app firewall. It does not require an account, does not use advertising or analytics SDKs, and does not upload your personal data to Cortavia servers. Your app list, settings, and block rules stay on your phone. Your internet traffic is filtered locally and never routed through our servers.

Data we collect

We do not collect, sell, or share personal information such as your name, email, phone number, contacts, location, photos, or browsing history.

The following information is processed only on your device and is not transmitted to Cortavia:

  • Names, icons, and package identifiers of installed apps (to let you block or allow internet)
  • Your block/allow rules, language preference, and firewall settings
  • Local VPN status needed to enforce those rules

Permissions

  • VPN service: Creates a local VPN on the device to block internet for selected apps. This is not a commercial VPN. Traffic is not sent to a remote Cortavia VPN server.
  • Query installed apps: Lists apps on the device so you can choose which ones may use the internet.
  • Notifications and foreground service: Shows that the firewall is running while rules are enforced.
  • Boot completed: Optionally restores the local firewall after the phone restarts, if you enable that setting.
  • Internet: Declared as a standard Android permission. App Wall does not upload your personal data or app list to Cortavia.

Sharing and third parties

We do not share App Wall user data with third parties. The app does not include ads, crash-reporting, or analytics services that collect personal data. Google Play may process install and review data under Google's own policies when you download the app from Play.

Data retention and deletion

Because data stays on the device, uninstalling App Wall removes the app and its local settings. You can also turn the firewall off and clear rules inside the app at any time.

Children

App Wall is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date.
